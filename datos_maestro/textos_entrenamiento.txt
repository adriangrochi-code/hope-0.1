Paso 1: ⟦calc: 2 + 1⟧ → 3 (Adding the two equations eliminates y, allowing us to solve for x directly.)
Paso 2: ⟦calc: 6 / 3⟧ → 2 (Divide both sides of the equation by 3 to isolate x.)
Paso 3: ⟦calc: 2 - 1⟧ → 1 (Substituting x back into one of the original equations allows us to solve for y.)
