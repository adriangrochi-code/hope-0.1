Tema: ecuaciones lineales
Problema: Calcular la corriente que circula por un circuito si la resistencia es de 5 ohmios y la tensión es de 10 volts.
Datos: 10 V; 5 Ω
Plan: Aplicar la ley de Ohm, V = I * R, para despejar la corriente I.
Paso 1: Despejar I en la fórmula V = I * R. = I = V / R (La ley de Ohm establece que la tensión es igual a la corriente multiplicada por la resistencia. Para despejar la corriente, se divide ambos lados por R.)
Paso 2: Sustituir los valores dados en la fórmula I = V / R. = I = 10 V / 5 Ω (Se sustituyen los valores de tensión y resistencia en la fórmula despejada para la corriente.)
Paso 3: Realizar la división. = I = 2 A (La tensión dividida por la resistencia da el valor de la corriente en amperes.)
Resultado: 2 A
Comprobación: V = I * R => 2 A * 5 Ω = 10 V, lo cual coincide con los valores iniciales.
Errores frecuentes: Confundir la fórmula de Ohm (I = V/R) con V = I*R sin despejar correctamente.

Tema: systems of linear equations
Problema: Solve the system of linear equations: 3x + 2y = 8 and x + y = 3.
Datos: 3; 2; 8; 1
Plan: Use the elimination method to solve the system of equations. First, multiply the second equation by 2 so that the coefficients of y are the same in both equations. Then subtract the two equations to eliminate y and solve for x. Substitute the value of x back into one of the original equations to find y.
Paso 1: Multiply the second equation (x + y = 3) by 2 to get 2x + 2y = 6. = 2x + 2y = 6 (This step ensures that the coefficients of y are the same in both equations, allowing us to eliminate y when we subtract the equations.)
Paso 2: Subtract the new equation (2x + 2y = 6) from the first equation (3x + 2y = 8). = x = 2 (By subtracting the equations, we eliminate y and solve for x.)
Paso 3: Substitute x = 2 into the second original equation (x + y = 3) to find y. = y = 1 (This step allows us to find the value of y using the known value of x.)
Resultado: x = 2, y = 1
Comprobación: Substitute x = 2 and y = 1 into both original equations. For the first equation: 3(2) + 2(1) = 6 + 2 = 8, which matches. For the second equation: 2 + 1 = 3, which is correct.
Errores frecuentes: Forgetting to multiply the entire equation by 2 when using elimination; Mistakingly adding instead of subtracting equations
